About the artist

A practice built on curiosity.

Illustration, animation, design, and code: different ways into an idea, and different ways to make it felt.

Introduction

Duy Le

Duy Le is an illustrator and digital artist working across illustration, animation, design, and creative coding. He draws inspiration from Japanese-style animation and explores different media to find new ways to develop and communicate ideas.

Artist Statement

To be an artist is to take self-expression and the work of creation seriously. I want to convey thoughts and feelings through art like nothing else. Creative work allows me to improve my skills and build a life around something I find meaningful.

I’m into anime and fan culture, so I see myself in the art world down the road. I admire artists and their fan art about the worlds they love so much. This curiosity makes me wonder how they make their work and helps me see digital art as a possible path for me. By studying Digital Media Art, I can explore this interest by trying different media and learn more about myself.

Through taking ART 74 at SJSU, I learned digital imaging, vector illustration, web design, animation, and creative coding. Learning After Effects taught me to think about time and motion in art. In an animation project inspired by games, I created a fan animation of Cardcaptor Sakura flying on her wand. I drew a chibi-style character and combined it with the game UI. Adding projectiles and sound effects gave the impression that the artwork could be played. This challenge required me to work across programs, which caused problems with resolution, timing, and workflow. I learned to be patient with editing and take responsibility for bringing all parts of the work together.

Using p5.js, I created a construction robot with moving lights in ART 74. Because I was new to coding, I researched how functions worked and tested ideas. I learned that coding can make art functional and shape what an artwork does and how it can be explored. I also learned ways to create and present images with Photoshop, Illustrator, HTML, and CSS.

For ART 75, I created a video called Technology Paradoxes. Using patterns, warped faces, and unusual timing, I expressed my concept without narration. Planning each shot helped me understand how technical choices can support the idea I want to communicate. These experiences help me choose the medium that best expresses my ideas.

Studying art has taught me to be resilient and committed in the process. It can be intimidating to be around other super talented artists, but I want to keep my passion alive. With this BFA program I hope to hone my skills in digital illustration, play with different media, and create a sustainable creative practice. The important thing is to make art that makes me happy and connects the people around me.The next step is to build the confidence, skill and discipline for a future in Digital Media Art.
