About the artist

A practice built on curiosity.

Illustration, animation, design, and code: different ways into an idea, and different ways to make it felt.

01 / Introduction

Hello, I’m Duy.

I’m Duy Le, an illustrator and digital media artist working across illustration, animation, design, and creative coding. I enjoy moving between structured visual design and playful experimentation, using each medium to find a new way into an idea.

02 / Artist statement

Making room for the unexpected.

My practice explores how images change as they move through different tools and forms. Through drawing, animation, and code, I investigate mood, movement, and visual identity. I’m interested in the space between intention and accident: setting up a process, then seeing what unexpected marks or meanings emerge. Across my work, I aim to make digital processes feel tactile, expressive, and open to discovery.
